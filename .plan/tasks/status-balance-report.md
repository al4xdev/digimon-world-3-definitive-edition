About **4.1 points per TP whatever the trainer** (the three tiers are balanced on purpose), so the 245 TP of a level-50 run is about **1001 points** to spend. (The 5-round trainings from id 13 pay more per session but only come later and cost the same; left out here, so these numbers are the safe side.)

**Plan.** From the median 338 Wisdom you need 661 more: **161 TP (66% of the 245)**. The rest, 83 TP, buys ~340 points of Spirit (median 316 + 340 = 656) or Wind resist. For the best-growth species (p90 Wisdom 371) it is only 153 TP.

A 999 Wisdom is therefore **reachable at 50 but it consumes most of the TP**: Spirit and Wind end modest. The poison below uses three profiles: *casual* (Wis 250 / Spirit 250), *focused* (Wis 600 / Spirit 400) and *pure* (Wis 999 / Spirit 656, Wind resist ~300 -> affinity 90).

## 6. Would it be annoying?

Sleep and Confusion last at most 2 boss turns (`DEF_STATUS_BOSS_TURNS`) and **end on any direct hit** (`def_wake_on_hit`), so the control is for healing/setup, not for the damage turn. What can still go wrong:

- *casual*: best Sleep lands 20% normal / 48% staggered. Expected attempts 5.1 / 2.1. A sleeping boss heals 6% of its max HP per turn, so 2 turns asleep give it back 12%.
- *focused*: best Sleep lands 40% normal / 78% staggered. Expected attempts 2.5 / 1.3. A sleeping boss heals 6% of its max HP per turn, so 2 turns asleep give it back 12%.
- *pure*: best Sleep lands 63% normal / 78% staggered. Expected attempts 1.6 / 1.3. A sleeping boss heals 6% of its max HP per turn, so 2 turns asleep give it back 12%.
